# Design & Art

O\*NET-SOC 27-0000 — Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media Occupations.

Visual design, UI/UX mockups, illustration, audio/video, generative and
procedural art, branding — anything where the deliverable is primarily
visual, auditory, or aesthetic rather than functional.

**Typical requests:** mock up a UI, design a logo, generate a palette,
storyboard a video, produce generative art, lay out a poster or slide deck.

**Convention:** each piece of work gets its own subdirectory
(`categories/design-art/<project-name>/`) holding both source (SVGs, design
files, scripts that generate output) and rendered output. Cross-link to
`development/` when a design is implemented as part of a coded project
rather than duplicating it here.
