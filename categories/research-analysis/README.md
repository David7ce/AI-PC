# Research & Analysis

O\*NET-SOC 19-0000 — Life, Physical, and Social Science Occupations.

Investigating a question, comparing options, summarizing sources, analyzing
data — anything where the deliverable is a conclusion or a synthesis rather
than a running artifact.

**Typical requests:** compare tools/libraries before picking one, summarize
a set of documents, analyze a dataset, fact-check a claim against primary
sources, produce a decision brief.

**Convention:** each piece of research gets a dated subdirectory
(`categories/research-analysis/YYYY-MM-DD-topic/`) with a short findings
doc up top and any supporting data/notebooks alongside it. Findings decay —
note the date something was checked, since "true as of when" matters more
here than in most other categories.
