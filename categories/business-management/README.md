# Business & Management

O\*NET-SOC 11-0000 — Management Occupations;
13-0000 — Business and Financial Operations Occupations.

Planning, budgeting, scheduling, tracking — personal or organizational
admin work where the deliverable is a plan or a record, not code or prose
for its own sake.

**Typical requests:** build a budget, plan a project timeline, draft a
process/checklist, track expenses, prepare a decision for someone else to
approve.

**Convention:** prefer plain, durable formats (Markdown tables, CSV) over
tool-specific formats, since the point is a record you can reopen years
later without needing the tool that made it.
